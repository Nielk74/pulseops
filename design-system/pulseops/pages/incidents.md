# Incidents Page Overrides

> **PROJECT:** PulseOps
> **Updated:** 2026-08-10
> **Page Type:** Incident triage workspace

## Summary

- Keep incident pressure above the feed: open count, high-priority count,
  affected entities, and an exact-value severity bar list.
- Bar color supplements the written severity and exact count; it never carries
  meaning alone.

## Incident cards

- Use a compact icon-and-severity top row and status-and-arrow bottom row on
  mobile. At desktop widths, those elements become one scan-friendly row.
- Keep the entire card as the detail target, retain visible focus, and use the
  shared modal route when the incident points to a supported entity.
